Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about shopping at Smart Garments

What are the delivery charges across Pakistan?
We offer free nationwide shipping on all orders above PKR 3,000. For orders below PKR 3,000, a standard shipping fee of PKR 200 applies.
How does Cash on Delivery (COD) work?
With Cash on Delivery, you can inspect your parcel upon delivery and pay the exact order amount in cash directly to our courier partner at your doorstep.
What is your return and exchange policy?
We offer a 7-day easy return and exchange policy for unworn items with original tags intact. Contact our support team to initiate an exchange.
How can I track my online order?
Once your order is dispatched, you will receive an SMS confirmation with your tracking number and courier details to monitor your delivery status.
